Steel Types: The Tanks of Pokemon

Why Steel Pokemon have the highest defense in the game, what types they pair with, and the tradeoff they make for their armor.

I keep hearing that Steel types are the best defensive Pokemon. Is that actually true?
Let me check the average Defense stat for each type across over 1,300 Pokemon.
It's not even close. Steel types average 113 Defense - that's 17 points higher than the next best type (Rock at 97). For context, that gap is like the difference between a decent defender and a top-tier wall. Steel types are literally built different.
There are 96 Steel Pokemon in the database. What types do they pair with most often?
Great question - type pairings matter a lot for coverage and weaknesses. Let me break it down.
14 Steel types are pure Steel with no secondary type. The most common pairing is Steel/Psychic (12 Pokemon) - think Metagross, Bronzong, and Jirachi. Steel/Fairy (9) gives you defensive powerhouses like Mawile. Steel/Dragon (8) includes box legendaries like Dialga. The rarest? Steel/Fire and Steel/Water with only 1 each - Heatran and Empoleon.
But there has to be a downside to all that defense, right?
Absolutely. Let me show you the Defense vs Speed tradeoff for non-legendary Steel Pokemon.
See that inverse relationship? The tankiest Steel types are glacially slow. Steelix and Aggron have 200+ Defense but only 30-50 Speed. They hit like trucks and take hits like bunkers, but they're always moving last. The few fast Steel types - Durant at 109 Speed, Lucario at 90 - sacrifice defense to get there. You can have armor or agility, but not both.
Who are the strongest Steel Pokemon overall?
Let me rank them by Base Stat Total - the sum of all six stats.
Three Pokemon tie at 700 BST: Zacian, Zamazenta (both legendaries), and Mega Metagross (the only non-legendary to reach that tier). Metagross is ridiculous - Steel/Psychic typing, 145 Attack, 150 Defense, and it's actually fast for a Steel type at 110 Speed. Among regular Pokemon, Aggron (630), Lucario (625), and Steelix (610) lead the pack. Steel types dominate the power rankings because their defensive bulk lets them survive long enough to deal massive damage.
