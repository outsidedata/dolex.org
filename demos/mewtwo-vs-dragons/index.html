Mewtwo vs Dragons: Who's Really Stronger?

Pitting the iconic Psychic legendary against Dragon-type powerhouses — base stats, Mega forms, and who wins the speed war.

How does Mewtwo stack up against legendary Dragon types?
Let me pull the base stat totals for Mewtwo and the legendary dragons.
Interesting — Mewtwo ties with the classic dragon legendaries at 680 BST. Rayquaza, Dialga, Palkia, Giratina, Reshiram, Zekrom... they're all in the same tier. Eternatus actually edges them out at 690, and the newer gen 9 legendaries Koraidon/Miraidon sit slightly lower at 670.
But same total stats doesn't mean same power. How do Mewtwo and Rayquaza compare stat for stat?
Great question. Same total, completely different builds.
Rayquaza is the physical attacker with 150 Attack vs Mewtwo's 110. But Mewtwo dominates in Speed — 130 vs 95. That's a massive 35-point gap. In competitive Pokemon, speed often determines who attacks first, and Mewtwo outspeeds almost every dragon legendary. Their Special Attack is nearly tied, with Mewtwo at 154 and Rayquaza at 150.
What about Mega Evolution? Which form is the strongest?
This is where things get wild. The Mega forms create an elite tier that towers above everything else.
Mega Mewtwo X, Mega Mewtwo Y, and Mega Rayquaza all hit 780 BST — tied for the highest in the entire franchise. Ultra Necrozma comes close at 754. Mewtwo is the only Pokemon with TWO forms in the 780 club.
So which Mega form is actually the best?
They're optimized for completely different roles. Let me break down the stat distributions.
Mega Mewtwo X is a physical monster with 190 Attack — the highest in the game. Mega Mewtwo Y goes all-in on Special Attack at 194 and is the fastest of the three at 140 Speed. Mega Rayquaza is the most balanced at 180/180 Attack/Sp.Atk. For competitive play: Mega Mewtwo Y is often considered the most dangerous because it outspeeds everything and hits like a nuclear bomb with special moves. But Mega Rayquaza doesn't need a held item to Mega Evolve, giving it more flexibility.
Bottom line — does Mewtwo beat the dragons?
In raw stats, it's a tie at base form and Mega form. But Mewtwo has one critical advantage the dragons don't: versatility. It has two Mega forms covering both physical and special attacking, plus that signature Speed stat. Dragons hit hard, but Mewtwo hits first — and in Pokemon, that's often all that matters. The Psychic type also gives it neutral or super-effective coverage against most dragons (except Steel-types like Dialga). So yeah — Mewtwo was designed to be the ultimate Pokemon, and even decades later, the dragons still can't quite catch it.
