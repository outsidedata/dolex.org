The Underrated Poison Types

Exploring why Poison is one of the most overlooked types despite having iconic Pokemon like Gengar and Eternatus.

I want to explore poison type Pokemon. How many are there compared to other types?
Let me pull up the type distribution. Looking at base forms only (excluding alternate forms like Megas), there are 42 Pokemon with Poison as their primary type.
That puts Poison in the bottom third of all types by count. Water dominates with 135 Pokemon, followed by Normal (119) and Grass (106). Poison is actually tied with Fighting and Ground at around 42-43 each. It's not a rare type, but definitely not as common as the big three.
What types do they usually pair with? I feel like I see a lot of Poison/Grass combos.
Your instinct is spot on. Let me show you the type pairings.
Pure Poison types are actually the most common (16), but Grass is the top secondary type with 14 Pokemon. Bug comes in third with 12. This makes sense thematically - venomous plants and poisonous insects are classic natural pairings. The rarer combos like Poison/Fairy (just 1) and Poison/Psychic (1) tend to be the more exotic designs.
How do poison types compare to other types in terms of overall strength?
Here's where it gets interesting. If we rank types by average Base Stat Total:
Poison ranks 14th out of 18 types with an average BST of 426. Dragon leads at 490, followed by Steel at 477. Poison sits in the lower tier alongside Water, Grass, and Normal. But here's the catch - BST averages are skewed by how many early-game Pokemon a type has. Poison has classics like Weedle and Ekans pulling down the average.
What about their individual stats? Are poison types specialists or generalists?
Great question. Let me compare Poison's stat profile against some of the elite types.
Poison types are the ultimate generalists - no major strengths, no major weaknesses. Compare that to Fighting (huge Attack, terrible Sp. Atk), Steel (massive Defense, low Speed), or Psychic (dominant Sp. Atk). Dragon excels across the board, which is why it's the top type. Poison's balanced profile means they're versatile but rarely best-in-class at anything.
